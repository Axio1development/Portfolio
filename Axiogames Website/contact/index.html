FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Why do I need to sign in to contact you?

Signing in with Discord stops spam and lets us reply to the right person. You do not need to link Roblox just to send a message.

How do I report a scammer?

Use the Report a Scam form on the Scam Logs page. You need to be verified with Discord and Roblox linked.

Where do I check my report?

On the Scam Logs page after signing in, on your Account page, or by running /report-status with the Axio Community Bot in our Discord server.

How do I apply for a job?

Open positions are listed on the Jobs page. Pick one and apply while signed in and verified.
