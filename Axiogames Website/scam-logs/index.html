How It Works

Every report follows the same steps so the log stays fair and accurate.

STEP 01

Submit

A verified member reports a Roblox user with as much evidence as possible.

STEP 02

Investigate

Scam Investigation staff review the evidence and look for matching reports.

STEP 03

Confirm

Only confirmed scammers are published, with their Roblox username and user ID.

STEP 04

Appeal

If someone is listed in error, they can open a ticket in Discord to appeal.

05

Stay Safe

Simple habits that stop most scams before they start.

TIP 01

Never share your login

Staff will never ask for your password or login codes.

TIP 02

Check the link

Look closely at the address before you sign in. Fake sites copy the real ones.

TIP 03

Be wary of rushing

Scammers create urgency. Take your time and ask questions.

TIP 04

Keep proof

Save screenshots of every deal. They matter if something goes wrong.

TIP 05

Check the log

Search a username here before you trade with someone new.
