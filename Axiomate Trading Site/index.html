      Axiomate Trading is a simulation / game. Every company, price and headline is randomly generated. No real money or securities are involved. Shortcuts: B/S side · 1–5 timeframe · ↑/↓ symbol · Space pause

      Welcome to Axiomate Trading

      A realistic stock-market simulator. You start with $100,000 in virtual cash. Every company, price and headline is made up.

      • The market runs on a clock. Each trading day goes 9:30 to 16:00. At 1× speed a full day takes about 20 minutes, and you can speed it up to 60×. Overnight, prices can gap up or down.
      • Prices move together. Stocks follow the overall market and their sector, then react to their own news. Volatility comes in bursts.
      • Earnings days (shown under each stock) can move a stock 5–15% at the open.
      • Market orders fill against the order book. Big orders eat through several price levels, so you pay slippage.
      • Limit orders wait for your price. Stop orders trigger a market order when the price breaks through. Add a take-profit or stop-loss to any entry.
      • Short selling and 2× margin are allowed. If your equity falls below 25% of your positions' value, you get a margin call and positions are closed automatically.
      • Commission is $0.005/share (min $1) for stocks and 0.10% for crypto.
      • Six AI traders run their own strategies in the same market. Try to beat them on the Leaderboard.
