FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Axiomate a real bank?

No. Axiomate Bank is a product concept built to show what a modern banking experience can look like. It is not a chartered bank, is not FDIC insured, and does not hold or move real money. Please don't enter real financial information.

So what actually works?

Nearly everything you'd use day to day: sign-up and sign-in, balances, transaction history with search and filters, transfers, sending money to other members, bill pay with scheduling, mobile check deposits, card controls, savings goals, statements, CSV export, alerts and security settings. It all runs on simulated money.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. Choose "Try the live demo" to get a temporary guest profile with sample accounts. Guest profiles are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Can I buy things with the card?

No. Cards in Axiomate are display-only and can't be used for purchases anywhere. Card numbers are never generated in full.

How do I delete my profile?

Go to Settings → Close profile inside the app. Your profile and all its simulated data are deleted immediately.
