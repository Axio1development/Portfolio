# // Axiomate Auto planning

(Please note more was added to the site, but not in full planning due to new ideas) 

Axiomate auto will have a learning system into it with a A --> F Grading system this would help the teachers properly grade someone, there will also be a % for the grade incase thats easier for some people. 

The first area will be the dashboard area and for the dashboard area there will be a car obviously because its a automotive learning site. below that will be the 3 boxes (Available courses, levels completed, and overall progress) below that will be "Find your next level" every course will start in beginner then move to intermediate then advanced then completed after for the total grade. 

Next area will be the course library and for this library there will be 16 lessons, the lessons will be

- Engine Fundamentals
- Electrical essentials 
- Braking Systems
- Engine Cooling
- Engine Repair
- Hvac systems 
- ADAS
- Electrical diagnostics
- Sound Systems
- Fuel Systems
- Diesels systems
- Sensors and signals
- Transmissions
- Steering & Suspension
- Hybrid & Ev Awareness
- Vehicle networks

Each course will have 16 questions, a person must answer all of them to get the final grade.

Next area will be my grades, this is where the student can view how many courses were graded and lessons submitted. 

Next area will be your classroom which is where the teacher account gives you a code that you put in the join the class.
